# Notice

DRAFT FOR HUMAN REVIEW.

## What these files record

This is an observational record of machine outputs. Every quoted passage is
text that a consumer AI assistant produced in response to a query, captured on
a stated date by the collecting analyst.

## Nothing here is verified as true

The recorded outputs contain claims about prices, services, clinical
capabilities, staffing, locations and comparative standing. None of it has been
checked. Machine-generated text of this kind is known to contain errors,
including confident and specific ones. Some of the analyst notes flag
suspected errors, but the absence of such a flag is not a finding that a
passage is correct.

Read every quoted passage as evidence of what an assistant said, never as
evidence that what it said is so.

## No statement about any organization is made or endorsed

The publisher makes no statement about any organization appearing in these
files, and does not adopt, endorse or stand behind any claim contained in the
recorded outputs. Reproducing a machine's assertion about a company is not
asserting it. Where an output praises or criticizes an organization, that is
the machine's output and is recorded for study.

## Nothing here rates, ranks or assesses any provider

These files do not rate, rank, score or assess any provider, and they are not a
directory, a recommendation or a guide to choosing care. The numeric values in
the analyst notes describe how prominently an assistant surfaced an
organization in its answer. They say nothing about that organization's quality,
safety, competence or standing. They are measurements of assistant behavior,
not of providers.

Nothing here is medical advice.

## Corrections

Corrections are welcome, including from organizations that believe an output
recorded here misdescribes them. A correction cannot change what an assistant
returned, since that is the observation. It can be recorded alongside the
observation, and it can correct the coding, the dating or the preparation.
